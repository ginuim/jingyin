## 简体中文

### 名称

镜隐

### 副标题

本地视频隐私打码与变音

### 宣传文本

视频全程在设备本地处理：像素化、模糊、手动遮盖、静音与变音，无需上传原视频。

### 描述

镜隐是一款端侧视频隐私处理工具。原视频、识别数据和导出结果都在你的设备上处理，
无需上传服务器。

主要功能：

• 从相册或“文件”导入视频  
• 对人物、人脸、宠物或手动椭圆/矩形区域添加遮盖  
• 支持隐私级像素化、视觉模糊和 ASCII 效果  
• 手动拖动、缩放遮盖区域，并在时间轴上调整关键帧  
• 保留原声、静音导出或改变音高  
• 导出时清除位置、设备和拍摄时间等来源元数据  
• 保存到相册或通过系统分享面板发送

免费版可以完整编辑和预览，导出视频前 30 秒，最高 720p，不添加水印。一次买断永久版
后可解除时长和分辨率限制。没有订阅，也没有广告。

首个版本专注于可靠处理短视频，支持最长 5 分钟、最大 1 GB 的视频文件。

## English

### Name

lenshide

### Subtitle

On-device video privacy

### Promotional Text

Process videos entirely on your device with pixelation, blur, manual masks, mute, and pitch shift. Your original video is never uploaded.

### Description

lenshide is an on-device video privacy tool. Original videos, recognition data, and exports are
processed on your device and are never uploaded to a server.

Key features:

• Import videos from Photos or Files  
• Cover people, faces, pets, or manually placed ellipse and rectangle areas  
• Choose privacy-focused pixelation, visual blur, or an ASCII effect  
• Move and resize masks, then adjust keyframes on the timeline  
• Keep original audio, export silently, or shift voice pitch  
• Remove source location, device, and capture-time metadata during export  
• Save to Photos or share through the system share sheet

The free version includes full editing and preview. Exports are limited to the first 30 seconds at
up to 720p, with no watermark. A one-time Lifetime Access purchase removes the duration and
resolution limits. There are no subscriptions and no ads.

The first version focuses on reliable short-video processing and supports videos up to 5 minutes
and files up to 1 GB.

## App Review Notes（英文，可直接粘贴）

lenshide does not require an account or sign-in.

All video frames, recognition data, audio processing, and exports are processed locally on the
device. The app does not upload the user's original video or export to a server.

Free users can use the complete editor and preview. Export is enforced inside the processing
pipeline to the first 30 seconds at up to 720p, with no watermark.

The app offers one Non-Consumable in-app purchase:

- Product ID: `com.reaidea.jingyin.lifetime`
- Display name: Lifetime Access
- This is a one-time purchase, not a subscription.

To test the purchase:

1. Import a video from Photos or Files.
2. Complete editing and proceed to Export.
3. Tap Unlock on the export screen.
4. On the Lifetime Access screen, tap the purchase button showing the localized App Store price.
5. Use an App Review Sandbox account to complete or cancel the purchase.

To test Restore Purchases:

1. Open Settings.
2. Under Lifetime Access, tap Restore Purchases.

The editor supports original audio, silent export, and offline pitch shifting. The first release
accepts video files up to 5 minutes and 1 GB. No demo account is required.

The development-only `-storekitUnlocked` launch argument is not part of the Release/App Review
workflow and does not grant production entitlement. Release entitlement is determined from
current StoreKit 2 transactions.
